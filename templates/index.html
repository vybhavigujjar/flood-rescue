Immediate Emergency Steps
  • Call 112 (National Emergency) or 108 (Ambulance).
  • Move to higher ground immediately. Do not wait for water levels to rise.
Floodwater Safety
  • Never walk, swim, or drive through flowing floodwaters. Just 15 cm of rapid water can sweep an adult away.
  • Stay clear of submerged electrical fixtures and downed power lines. Switch off main breakers if dry.
Person Caught in Water
  • Do not enter the swift current. Throw a buoyant rope, float, or container and sound the alarm.
  • Once retrieved, prevent hypothermia, keep them dry, check breathing, and seek medical help.
Wounds & Contamination
  • Press firmly with a sterile bandage or clean cloth to arrest bleeding.
  • Floodwater carries sewage and toxic pathogens. Disinfect wounds promptly with safe water.
Safe Drinking Water
  • Assume municipal tap water and wells are contaminated. Drink bottled water or boil for at least 1 minute.
